# Philosophy

## The Problem

Our generation rarely settles. We rent, move, study abroad, switch cities. We own less and less, but still want beauty in our lives.

The problem: most beautiful things are too expensive, and most cheap things aren't worth keeping.

## The Belief

Good objects don't belong to one person forever. They should move between people. Like stories. Like lives.

We don't need to own everything. We just need to experience what matters, and then let it go.

## Our Worldview

- Ownership is not always the answer. Experience can be.
- Good things deserve to be cherished more than once.
- This doesn't require a high budget or passing an aesthetic exam.

## What We're Not

- Not elitist (you don't need to "understand" to appreciate)
- Not gatekeeping (no "if you know you know" energy)
- Not performative (no loud logos, no trying too hard)

## What We Believe

- A beautiful life doesn't require financial suffering.
- Taste doesn't require an exam.
- Secondhand doesn't mean settling.
- Quality living can be smarter and more circular.
