# Brand Story

## Short Version (30 seconds)

I made a space.

I go to secondhand markets and online platforms. I pick things — one by one. Only what I think is beautiful, and smartly priced.

Cups. Lamps. Scarves. Small bags. Candles. Things you actually use.

You can buy them. Or just "experience them for a while."

Don't like it? Moving? Tired of it? Send it back. Let it keep moving.

This is not a secondhand store.

This is Delta — a circular space curated by one person's taste.

Beautiful things should circulate. That's all.

## Long Version

I started this because I am that person — moving often, on a budget, but unwilling to settle for ugly things.

I hunt on secondhand platforms. I pick what I genuinely love — quiet, smart, worth circulating, usable.

A cup from Japan. A lamp from a flea market. A wool scarf that will last ten years.

Price doesn't matter. Some things are $5. Some are $100. The only thing that matters is whether they fit the framework.

You can buy it. Or you can just borrow it for a season.

When you're done — moving, bored, ready for something new — send it back. We'll give you credit. You come back.

What comes back, we clean and resell. Part of that profit goes to charity.

You keep beautiful things moving. We do something good with it.

Not a secondhand store. Delta is a circular space with a worldview.
