# Charity Mechanism

## How It Works

When an object comes back and gets sold again, a portion of that profit goes to charity.

## Which Charity

We focus on specific, tangible causes — currently animal rescue and children's literacy. We keep it concrete so you know exactly where the money goes.

## Transparency

We publish donation records every quarter. You can see what we gave, where it went, and when.

## Why We Do This

Returning something shouldn't feel like "I made a mistake." It should feel like "I participated in something good."

You let the object circulate. We donate on your behalf.

It's not about being saints. It's about making circulation — and letting go — feel meaningful.
