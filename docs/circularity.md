# The Circular Mechanism

## How It Works

You buy something. You own it. You use it.

When you're ready to let it go — because you're moving, because your taste changed, because you just don't need it anymore — you send it back.

We give you credit toward your next find.

The object gets cleaned, restaged, and finds a new home.

## Why This Exists

Most of us don't want to hoard. We want to experience good things without being tied to them forever.

This is not a rental. You own it while you have it. But you have an exit — a graceful, easy way to let it go when it's time.

## Why Delta

A river delta is never still. Water arrives, splits, merges, finds new paths, keeps moving.

Nothing stays. Everything circulates.

That's what we want for objects. And for the people who use them.

## What We Believe About Ownership

- You don't need to own everything forever
- Experience is valid
- Letting go is not failure
- Good objects deserve multiple lives
