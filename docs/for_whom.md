# Who This Is For

## Typical Profile

- Age 20-30
- Student, creative professional, or frequent mover
- Limited budget, but sensitive to aesthetics
- Doesn't want to hoard, doesn't want to settle
- Doesn't know where to find good-looking, affordable things

## The Real Identity

You are the typical user. That's not a coincidence. That's the advantage.

Delta exists because someone needed it.

## A User Might Say

> "I want to live with taste, but I don't want to spend a fortune."

> "I move too often to accumulate things."

> "I want to experience good design without being tied to it forever."

## Not For Everyone

Delta is not for collectors. Not for people who need perfect condition. Not for people who want loud logos or status symbols.

Delta is for people who want a beautiful life, without trying too hard.
